    How It Works

    Four simple steps from sign-up to fresh water at your door. No contracts, no hassle — just pure water on your schedule.

    The Process

    Four Steps to Pure Water

    1

    Choose Your Plan

    Select your water type (RO, Alkaline, or Hydrogen), jug size (1–5 gallon), and delivery frequency. Mix and match — no rigid bundles.

    2

    Confirm & Schedule

    Contact us to confirm your order. We'll set up your delivery schedule — weekly, bi-weekly, or on-demand. We'll confirm the time window that works for you.

    3

    Fresh Jugs Arrive

    We deliver fresh, purified water right to your door and swap out your empties at the same time. No stacking empties, no trips to the store.

    4

    Repeat on Schedule

    Your delivery repeats automatically on your chosen schedule. Need to skip or adjust? Just call or text us — no penalties, no hoops.

    Quick Answers

    Common Questions

    Do I need to be home for delivery?

    No. Just leave your empty jugs at the door and we'll swap them with full ones. You don't need to be present — though we're happy to meet you if you're around.

    Can I skip a delivery?

    Yes, anytime. Call or text us at least 24 hours before your scheduled delivery and we'll skip that week at no charge. No fees, no questions asked.

    How do jug deposits work?

    Each 5-gallon jug carries a $5 refundable deposit. When we deliver, we collect the deposit for new jugs. When we pick up empties, we credit the deposit back to your account.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Absolutely. No contracts, no cancellation fees, no minimum commitment. Call us and we'll stop deliveries effective immediately and refund any jug deposits.
