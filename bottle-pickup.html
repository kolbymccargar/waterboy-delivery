    Return Options

    How To Return Your Jugs

    01

    Swap on Delivery

    FREE

    The easiest option. On your next delivery day, just leave your empty jugs outside. Our driver picks them up and replaces them with fresh full ones in a single visit. Happens automatically — no scheduling needed.

    02

    Drop Off at Store

    FREE

    Bring your empty jugs to our store at 7119 Elk Grove Blvd, Elk Grove, CA 95758. Walk-ins welcome during business hours. Deposits are credited to your account same day.

    03

    Scheduled Home Pickup

    $4.99

    Need us to come by outside of a regular delivery? We'll schedule a dedicated jug pickup at your door for a small $4.99 convenience fee. Contact us to set it up.

    Deposit Policy

    The $5 Deposit
    Explained

    $5

    Per Jug, Fully Refundable

    Every jug carries a $5 deposit, charged when you receive it. The full amount is refunded when the jug is returned in good condition. Deposits are tracked automatically on your account — no need to keep receipts.

    0

    Returned if Lost or Damaged

    Lost or damaged jugs forfeit the deposit — it helps us cover the cost of replacement. If a jug was damaged in transit (not by you), contact us and we'll make it right. Normal wear and tear is never penalized.
