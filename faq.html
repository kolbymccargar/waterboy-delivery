    Everything you need to know about Waterboy Delivery — pricing, pickups, deposits, and more.

    We offer three types of purified water: Reverse Osmosis (RO) — stripped of 99% of contaminants for maximum purity; Alkaline — pH 8.5+ for a smoother, mineral-rich taste; and Hydrogen — infused with molecular hydrogen for advanced hydration. All water is available in 1, 2, 3, and 5-gallon jugs.
    Delivery fees depend on your distance from our store at 7119 Elk Grove Blvd:

    Zone 1 (0–3 miles): FREE
    Zone 2 (3–6 miles): $4.99
    Zone 3 (6–9 miles): $9.99

    Use the zone checker to find your zone instantly.
    Each water type goes through a different filtration and treatment process. RO water requires our 4-stage reverse osmosis system. Alkaline water adds an ionization step. Hydrogen water requires a specialized infusion process. The extra equipment and processing time for each upgrade is reflected in the price difference.
